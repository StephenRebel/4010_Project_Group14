\begin{abstract}
  The problem of training a Reinforcement Learning Agent to produce short musical compositions from scratch will be addressed. 
  To address this problem, we designed a Gymnasium environment to train an RL agent by generating musical 
  compositions and returning feedback through a reward computed over the result. 
  We then compared multiple different RL methods (Deep Q Networks) and compared the overall outcomes. 
  This agent was trained from scratch, learning entirely from its experience in our environment and guided by our reward function. 
  Thus, it is pivotal to produce a meaningful and useful result to design an effective reward function
\end{abstract}


\section{Introduction}
Our project primarily focuses on training a Reinforcement Learning agent that is able to produce a short, 4-bar musical piece from scratch. The main challenge that we faced, however, lies with creating musically coherent sequences. This was a challenge as we had to take into account multiple dimensions of musical quality, such as rhythm, melody, harmony, and structural progression. To elaborate, the overall musical quality is affected by each note chosen, through its interactions with both previous and subsequent notes. Music generation differs from RL applications as it requires the agent to be able to handle multiple competing objectives. Our approach is different from other existing work because we teach and train agents without a form of pre-training on musical datasets or a pre-existing core foundation, so we essentially train them from scratch. The agent learns through trial and error, guided by our custom reward function, which encodes musical principles and aspects of music theory.
